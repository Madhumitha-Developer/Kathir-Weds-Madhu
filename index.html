Getting there

The Venue

Reception & Muhurtham

GS Resort

ECR / SH 49, Therkupoigainallur, Nagapattinam, Tamil Nadu 611111 · Plus code MRXM+VF3

  • By air: Tiruchirappalli International Airport (TRZ) is about 150 km away — roughly 3½ hours by taxi via Thanjavur and Thiruvarur.
  • By train: Nagapattinam Junction is about 10 km north; Velankanni station is a short auto ride away.
  • By bus: Frequent town buses run between Nagapattinam new bus stand and Velankanni along SH 49 — ask to stop at GS Resort.
  • By road: On the East Coast Road (SH 49) between Nagapattinam and Velankanni, opposite Hotel Carnival. Parking is available at the resort.
